# Fieldkit

A client-side mod for Forge 1.20.1 and Fabric 1.21.1.

- **M** opens the Fieldkit screen:
  - **Map**: in singleplayer, drawn from your world's real generator (modded biomes included), with structures, spawn and you. On servers, the chunks you've loaded this session.
  - **Seed finder**: ask for biomes and structures near 0, 0; it searches seeds with your world's own generator settings. Copy a seed or preview it on the map.
  - **Console**: the game log, filtered and searchable, plus an analysis (mod conflicts, missing textures, lag, memory) and live FPS/TPS/memory.
  - **Combat**: this session's kills, deaths, K/D, damage dealt and taken, and a fight log.
  - **Info**: game, Java, memory, GPU, world and load times.
  - **Settings**.
- **HUD**: biome minimap with mobs and players, coordinates/biome/time, a combat panel (armour durability, totems, arrows, CPS, ping, effects, fight totals), target info under the crosshair, and warnings when hostile mobs close in or players come near.
- **Alerts**: errors in the log, server lag, low memory, FPS drops, chat mentions and keywords, joins/leaves/deaths, load times.

Servers can switch off player radar the same way they do for Xaero's/JourneyMap (the usual fair-play codes); Fieldkit honours them.
